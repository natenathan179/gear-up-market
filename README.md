The SEO Strategy I'd Use

Don't try to make the homepage rank for every gym-equipment keyword.

Instead:

Homepage → "Gym Equipment Marketplace"

Category pages → "Commercial Gym Equipment," "Used Gym Equipment," "Home Gym Equipment," etc.

Product pages → Individual equipment/model keywords

Brand pages → Brand + equipment keywords

Location pages → Location + gym equipment

Blog → Informational/long-tail searches

That creates a scalable SEO silo + marketplace architecture capable of targeting thousands of relevant search queries without keyword stuffing.

Most importantly: don't buy cheap automated backlinks or create hundreds of low-quality AI pages just to inflate the site. For a marketplace like this, I'd focus on high-quality product/category content, genuine seller listings, internal linking, structured data, digital PR, relevant fitness-industry backlinks, and strong technical SEO.
